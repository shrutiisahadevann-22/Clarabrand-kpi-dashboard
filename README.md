# CLARA Brand Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

A self-initiated Power BI KPI tracker that tests a brand strategy against its own targets.

## Why I built this
CLARA is a fictional electrolyte water brand I worked on in my Digital Marketing Technology and Management module at Warwick Business School. The coursework ended with a strategy report and a set of targets, but it never tested whether the strategy would meet them.

After submission, nobody asked me to continue. I built this dashboard on my own to answer the business questions properly, as if CLARA were a live brand, and to practise turning a strategy into measurable KPIs.

## Business questions
The dashboard answers five questions:
1. Is the transparency content building trust?
2. Is trust turning into relationship and advocacy?
3. Which channel earns its budget?
4. Online versus retail: where do sales come from?
5. Which of the four influencers gives the best returns per post?

## Data
All data is **simulated**. CLARA is a fictional brand, and the targets come from my own strategy report. The findings below show how the tracker works, not real market results.

## What I built
- A **2-page Power BI KPI tracker**
- **DAX measures** for ROI, bounce rate and sign-up rate
- Targets taken from my strategy report, so each KPI can be compared with plan

## Key findings
| Channel | Result |
|---|---|
| **SEO & Blog** | The only channel with a positive return: **+154% ROI** |
| **Influencers** | The biggest loss: **-56% ROI** |

**What this means:** if the simulated data held in practice, budget should shift from influencer spend towards SEO and blog content. This is exactly the kind of decision a KPI tracker is built to support.

## Limitations
- Simulated data means the percentages aren't real market performance
- The value of this project is the method: framing business questions, defining KPIs, writing DAX, and reaching a recommendation
